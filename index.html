~ ❯ About

StarDalur is a small software house based in Kopavogur, Iceland. We deal in cross-platform mobile and web app development. Our expertise includes ticketing systems, custom admin panels, harnessing geolocation services and providing custom mapping solutions. We have a particular understanding of the tourism and wellness sectors. We have a passion for producing unique, eye-catching layouts and lean user interfaces. We can provide everything from photography and design to deployment & infrastructure management.
